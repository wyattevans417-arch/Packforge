Physics lab

Motion, forces & energy

Start with measurable quantities: how fast something moves, how its velocity changes, and how forces change motion.

F
VELOCITYv = Δx / Δt

Velocity is displacement divided by elapsed time. Direction matters.

ACCELERATIONa = Δv / Δt

Acceleration measures how quickly velocity changes.

NEWTON'S 2ND LAWF = ma

Net force equals mass times acceleration.

KINETIC ENERGYKE = ½mv²

Doubling speed makes kinetic energy four times larger.
