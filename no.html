How big is it?

Bars show approximate diameter relative to Jupiter, the largest planet.

Rocky worlds

Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars have solid surfaces made largely of rock and metal.

Giant planets

Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants. Uranus and Neptune are usually classified as ice giants because their interiors contain larger fractions of water, ammonia, and methane compounds.

Small-body regions

The asteroid belt lies mainly between Mars and Jupiter. Beyond Neptune, the Kuiper Belt contains icy worlds including Pluto.

Stars

A star is a self-gravitating ball of plasma powered for most of its life by nuclear fusion in its core.

Fusion

In a main-sequence star, hydrogen nuclei ultimately fuse into helium. The released energy supports the star against gravitational collapse.

Color means temperature

Blue-white stars have hotter surfaces than red stars. The Sun is a G-type star with a surface temperature around 5,800 K.

Stellar nurseries

Stars form when dense regions inside cold molecular clouds collapse under gravity and grow into protostars.

Chapter 3

Deep Space

Past the Solar System, distance becomes so enormous that astronomers commonly use light-years and parsecs instead of kilometers.

03
🌌

Galaxies

Galaxies are gravitationally bound systems of stars, gas, dust, dark matter, and stellar remnants. The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy.

Milky Way ≈ 100,000 light-years across
🕳️

Black holes

A black hole has gravity strong enough that, inside its event horizon, not even light can escape to the outside universe.

They can be stellar-mass or millions to billions of solar masses
☁️

Nebulae

Nebulae are clouds of gas and dust. Some are star-forming regions, while others are material expelled by dying stars or supernovae.

The Orion Nebula is a nearby stellar nursery
🪐

Exoplanets

Exoplanets orbit stars beyond the Sun. Astronomers find them through methods including transits, radial velocity, microlensing, and direct imaging.

Some systems contain multiple tightly packed worlds
Distance ladder

Click outward through the universe

Light-year

A light-year is a distance, not a time: the distance light travels through vacuum in one year, roughly 9.46 trillion kilometers.

Lookback time

Because light takes time to travel, distant objects are seen in the past. A galaxy 10 million light-years away is observed as it was about 10 million years ago.

Cosmic web

On the largest scales, galaxies and clusters trace enormous filaments and walls separated by comparatively empty voids.

Assembly began on a continuously inhabited orbital laboratory built through international cooperation.

NASA's rover landed in Gale Crater to investigate Mars' geology and past habitability.

2021

JWST launched to study the infrared universe, from nearby planetary systems to very distant galaxies.
